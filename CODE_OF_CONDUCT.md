# Code of Conduct

Memorica is built by people with different backgrounds, constraints, and levels of experience. We expect contributors to keep discussions respectful, specific, and oriented toward improving the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct about technical risks without making it personal.
- Assume good intent, but do not ignore harmful behavior.
- Give actionable feedback with enough context for someone to improve the work.
- Respect privacy and do not disclose secrets, private data, or security reports publicly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Dismissing security, accessibility, or reliability concerns without review.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or community.
